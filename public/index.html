logi-bot について

logi-bot とは

5ステップの対話とドリルダウンで、バラバラなアイデアからプレゼンの全体構想（合体ロボ図）を組み立てる思考整理パートナーアプリです。

各ステップで AI と一問一答しながら Mermaid 図を自動生成。最終的に「合体ロボ（全体シナリオ図）」が完成したら、Markdown としてエクスポートできます。

What is logi-bot?

logi-bot is a thinking-partner app that helps you build a complete presentation outline (the "Combo-Robot diagram") from scattered ideas through 5-step guided Q&A with AI.

Each step generates a Mermaid diagram automatically. Once all steps are complete, you can export the full scenario as Markdown.

Framework Credits & References

The thinking steps and "Combo-Robot Strategy" framework in this app are inspired by the work of Toyoshi Nagata. For a deeper dive into visual thinking techniques, please refer to the original book and article below.
